About me

I'm Harini, a UI/UX designer with a background in digital advertising and some hands-on experience with front-end development.

My interest in UX grew naturally from the work I was already doing. Over the years, I worked closely with websites, campaigns and performance data, and I became increasingly curious about how people interact with digital products — what catches their attention, where they struggle, and what makes an experience easier to use.

That curiosity eventually led me to UI/UX design. Today, I enjoy understanding a problem, exploring different possibilities and turning them into designs that are simple, clear and useful.

Having worked with both data and front-end development also influences the way I design. I think about not only how something looks, but how it works for the user and how it can work in the real world.
