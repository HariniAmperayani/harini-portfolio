InterviewOS

An AI-powered interview practice platform designed to help people feel more prepared and confident when interviewing for a new role.

My role

End-to-end Product Design

Process

Discovery · User flows · Experience design

Status

Design and development in progress

Problem

Job seekers may feel unprepared for interviews when they lack realistic practice and clear guidance on how to improve.

Goal

Create an interview practice experience that gives candidates useful feedback and a clear direction for improvement.

Intended Outcome

Candidates feel more prepared and confident when they enter a real interview.

My contribution

I'm leading the end-to-end product design, from discovery and user flows to the interview practice experience. Design and development are ongoing.
